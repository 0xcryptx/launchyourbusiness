Why it helps

Starting a business shouldn't feel like guesswork.

When you're starting out, it's hard to know what needs doing, in what order, and who to turn to. Most people end up piecing it together from Google searches and well-meaning advice.

My Business Start-Up Programme takes that pressure off. We'll build a plan around your business, and I'll help you put the pieces in place, from the very first decisions to getting in front of your first customers.
