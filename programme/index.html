The Programme

Everything in the right order, with someone in your corner.

I help people turn their skills and ideas into a business of their own. Here's what we'll work through together.

What's covered

Five building blocks for a business that's ready to launch

Every business is different, so we'll go at your pace and spend the most time where you need it.

01

Business structure

One of the first decisions you'll make is how to set up. We'll talk through what it means to be a sole trader versus forming a limited company, so you understand the differences before you choose.

  • Sole trader and limited company, side by side
  • What each option means for you day to day
02

Banking & insurance

Keeping business money separate and being properly covered are part of running things well. We'll get you organised for your business bank account and work out which types of cover you may need.

  • Getting ready to open a business bank account
  • Understanding the cover your business may need
03

Branding

Your brand is how people recognise you and remember you. Together we'll develop your business name, your visual identity, and the way you talk about what you offer.

  • Choosing your business name
  • Your look: logo, colours and style
  • How you describe what you do
04

Website design

Most customers will look you up before they get in touch. You'll get a professional website that explains clearly what you do and makes it easy for people to reach you.

  • A clean, professional site that works on phones
  • Clear information about your services
  • Simple ways for customers to contact you
05

Marketing & social media

A great business still needs people to know it exists. We'll decide who you want to reach, what to post, and how to get the word out when you launch.

  • Knowing who your customers are
  • What to post and where
  • A plan for launch day and beyond
